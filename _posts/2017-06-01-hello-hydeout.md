IoTime 2026 is held in conjunction with the **[27th IEEE International Symposium on a World of Wireless, Mobile and Multimedia Networks (WoWMoM 2026)](https://www.cs.unibo.it/wowmom2026/)** in **Bologna, Italy**.

**Workshop day:** Monday **16 June 2026**, **10:30–13:00** (morning). The program includes a welcome, a **keynote** by Prof. **Luca Bedogni** (*Beyond the Edge: Latency Management in Autonomous IoT Systems*), a short break, **four paper presentations**, and closing remarks including the BPA announcement.
